Licence to use Copernicus Products  ·  v1.2
============================================

This file is a POINTER, not the canonical text.

The Copernicus Products are licensed under the *Licence to use Copernicus
Products*, version 1.2 (or the version applicable when each individual product
is downloaded; CDS shows the current version on every dataset page).

The authoritative, legally-binding text MUST be obtained from the
European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF):

  Canonical URL (PDF):
    https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/api/v2/terms/static/licence-to-use-copernicus-products.pdf

  Dataset terms page (per dataset):
    https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/cdsapp#!/dataset/<dataset-name>?tab=overview

Summary of the key user obligations (NON-AUTHORITATIVE — for orientation only;
the text above is the authority):

  - Permitted: free use (commercial included), redistribution, modification,
    creation of derivative products.
  - Required: attribute the data producer (ECMWF) and the Copernicus
    Climate Change Service (C3S); cite the dataset DOI when publishing.
  - Required: pass these obligations on to recipients of any redistribution.
  - Forbidden: removing or hiding attribution; implying ECMWF / C3S endorsement
    of derived products.
  - Disclaimer: data are provided "as is"; users assume all risk.

Required citation for ERA5 monthly averaged data used in this repository:

  Hersbach, H., Bell, B., Berrisford, P., et al. (2023):
  ERA5 monthly averaged data on single levels from 1940 to present.
  Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) Climate Data Store (CDS).
  DOI: https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.f17050d7

If you redistribute / modify the CSV samples in
`example/capstone_5y_cds/data/`, you must:

  1. Keep the citation above visible in your derived work.

(copernicus-support@ecmwf.int).
